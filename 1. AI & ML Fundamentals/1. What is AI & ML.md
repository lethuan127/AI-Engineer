# What is AI & ML?

## Definitions

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** — the broad field of building systems that perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence: perception, reasoning, decision-making, language understanding.

**Machine Learning (ML)** — a subset of AI where systems **learn patterns from data** rather than being explicitly programmed with rules.

**Deep Learning (DL)** — a subset of ML using **neural networks with many layers** to learn complex patterns (especially in images, audio, text).

**Generative AI (GenAI)** — a subset of DL focused on **producing new content** (text, images, code, audio) rather than just classifying or predicting.

**Large Language Models (LLMs)** — generative AI models trained on text at massive scale, built on the **transformer** architecture.

## A Brief History

- **1950s–1980s — Symbolic AI**: hand-coded rules, expert systems, logic-based approaches. Worked for narrow problems but didn't scale.
- **1990s–2000s — Statistical ML**: SVMs, decision trees, random forests, kernel methods. ML moves from rules to data.
- **2012 — Deep Learning revolution**: AlexNet wins ImageNet by a huge margin using a deep CNN on GPUs. Era of scale begins.
- **2017 — Transformers**: ["Attention Is All You Need"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) introduces the transformer architecture — foundation of all modern LLMs.
- **2020s — Foundation models & GenAI**: GPT-3, GPT-4, Claude, Gemini. Pre-trained models that generalize across tasks; the AI Engineer role emerges.

## Symbolic vs. Statistical AI

| Aspect | Symbolic AI | Statistical / ML AI |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge source | Hand-coded rules | Learned from data |
| Strengths | Explainable, deterministic | Scales, handles fuzzy patterns |
| Weaknesses | Brittle, doesn't scale | Black box, needs data |
| Examples | Rule engines, Prolog | Neural nets, gradient boosting |

Modern AI is overwhelmingly statistical, but **symbolic + statistical hybrids** (e.g., agents using tools, structured outputs from LLMs) are increasingly important.

## Quick Code: ML in 5 Lines

The "hello world" of ML — predicting a flower species from measurements:

```python
from sklearn.datasets import load_iris
from sklearn.ensemble import RandomForestClassifier
from sklearn.model_selection import train_test_split

X, y = load_iris(return_X_y=True)
X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test = train_test_split(X, y, test_size=0.2)
model = RandomForestClassifier().fit(X_train, y_train)
print("Accuracy:", model.score(X_test, y_test))
```

That's it. Load data, split it, fit a model, evaluate. Most ML pipelines are elaborations of this skeleton.

## Takeaways

- AI ⊃ ML ⊃ DL ⊃ GenAI ⊃ LLMs — know the nesting.
- ML learns from **data**, not rules.
- LLMs are deep neural networks (transformers) trained at massive scale.
- The field shifted from rules → statistics → deep learning → foundation models.
