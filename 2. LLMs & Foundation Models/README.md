- [Test-Time Compute — Spending Inference to Buy Reasoning](Test-Time%20Compute%20—%20Spending%20Inference%20to%20Buy%20Reasoning.md) — the third scaling axis: spend more compute *per request* (more thinking tokens, more samples, more verification) to buy a better answer from a model whose weights never change. - [Diffusion LLMs for the Agent Loop — Parallel Decoding and the Speed–Quality Pareto](Diffusion%20LLMs%20for%20the%20Agent%20Loop%20—%20Parallel%20Decoding%20and%20the%20Speed–Quality%20Pareto.md) — the 2026 break from autoregression's sequential next-token floor: dLLMs denoise a whole masked sequence in parallel over ~8–16 fixed steps (bidirectional attention, runtime quality knob), hitting 1,000–2,000+ tok/s (Mercury 2, Gemini Diffusion, Seed Diffusion, LLaDA2.1). The speed–quality Pareto (wins on code/infilling/structured output, loses on reasoning/long-form), the different serving cost structure (no KV cache, no streaming, ~3–5× concurrency), and the heterogeneous verdict — route latency-bound inner-loop steps to diffusion, keep reasoning on AR.

The model lineup (Jamba, Nemotron-H, Nemotron 3 Super 1M-context, Qwen3.6), the attention-ratio knob, and why it matters to harness builders.
